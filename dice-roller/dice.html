🎲 Fun Dice Facts

  • Opposite sides of a standard six-sided die always add up to seven. One and six, two and five, three and four.
  • The oldest known dice date back over 5,000 years, found in Iran and Egypt. People have been rolling for chance since before written history.
  • A fair six-sided die gives each number a one in six chance, but roll two dice together and seven becomes the most likely total, since more combinations add up to it than any other number.
  • Casino dice are precision made to strict tolerances, often accurate to within a few thousandths of an inch, to guarantee a truly random roll.
  • The plural of die is technically dice, though in casual speech people often say die for both one and many.
