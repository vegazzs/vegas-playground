🌡️ Fun Temperature Facts

  • Celsius is built entirely around water: 0° is freezing, 100° is boiling. Simple, clean, and honestly, it's why almost the whole world uses it.
  • Fahrenheit's zero point came from a mixture of ice, water, and salt, the coldest Daniel Fahrenheit could reliably make in his lab back in 1724.
  • Fahrenheit originally set 96° as human body temperature, based on measuring himself. Turns out he was a little off. It's actually closer to 98.6°.
  • Kelvin has no negative numbers. Its zero point isn't arbitrary like the other two, it's absolute zero, the coldest anything in the universe can physically get.
  • 40° below zero is the one temperature where Celsius and Fahrenheit actually agree. Kelvin never joins that party, since it can't go negative at all.
