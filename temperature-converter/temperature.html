🌡️ Fun Temperature Facts

  • 40° below zero is the one temperature where Celsius and Fahrenheit actually agree.
  • 0 Kelvin is called absolute zero, the coldest anything can theoretically get.
  • Fahrenheit's inventor based his scale partly on his own body temperature, though it turned out to be slightly off.
  • Water freezes at 0°C, 32°F, and 273.15K. Same exact moment, three different numbers.
  • The Sun's surface is about 5,500°C, while its core reaches roughly 15 million°C.
